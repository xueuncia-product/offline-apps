## Everything the game needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

A pure, deterministic poker engine under a quiet, tactile interface. The sharp stuff runs deep; the surface stays calm.

### A cast you’ll get to know

Pip seats a fixed troupe of regulars, not random bots. Each weighs Monte-Carlo equity against pot odds through a personality of their own — they value-bet thin, float, barrel, and lay hands down. And they remember you: your reads on them build across sessions, like a real home game.

### The Daily Deal

One seeded tournament a day — everyone in the world plays the identical shuffle, and because the engine is open source, that's provable. Same cards, same opponents; your play makes the difference.
