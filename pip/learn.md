## Quick answers

-   ### [How often do you flop a set?](https://playpip.io/offline-apps/pip/learn/how-often-do-you-flop-a-set)
    
    About 11.8% of the time, or one flop in 8.5. All 19,600 flops a pocket pair can meet, graded, including the 48 that hand you a full house without a card of your rank in them.
    
-   ### [Three pair in Texas Hold’em](https://playpip.io/offline-apps/pip/learn/three-pair-in-texas-holdem)
    
    You can hold three pairs and you cannot play them: a hand is five cards, so the top two pairs play and the third is worth a kicker, one time in four. With every rank shape counted and two spots walked through.
